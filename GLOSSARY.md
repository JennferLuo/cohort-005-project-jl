# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and can ask for their money back shortly after buying.

## Language

**Refund**:
Money returned to a learner for a purchase, at the amount they paid.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, Cancel purchase

**Refund request**:
A learner's written ask for a Refund on a purchase, reviewed by hand. It is either Requested, Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, Reversal request, Cancellation request

**Refund window**:
The fourteen calendar days, counted from the day of purchase, during which a Refund request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, Cancellation period

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a learner may make a Refund request on a purchase right now, judged by the Refund window, how much of the course they have watched or completed, and whether the purchase is a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer
